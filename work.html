About

I have completed academic courses in Sound Design, with professional capabilities in full-pipeline audio production for moving images. Independently handled the entire sound design process for all my short films, including Foley recording, sound effect design, ambient background layering, and multi-track audio mixing. I specialize in using auditory atmosphere to match visual emotion and enhance narrative immersion.
