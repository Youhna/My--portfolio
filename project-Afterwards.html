Afterwards

Category: AIGC / Video  •  Year: 2025

The Concept

In an age where people increasingly rely on AI—tireless, compliant, never arguing—are we losing the capacity to face real intimacy? This two-minute short film, through symbols of a lone bird, a hand, vines, and a totemic figure, contrasts the comfort of seamless responses with the fragile tension of human love. Cool tones of blue and gray, interrupted by red lifelines, frame the question: Is a response without hesitation or friction still true understanding?

Visual narratives are not just about what you see, but what you feel. In this project, I explored the relationship between silence and noise using AI generation tools.

Tools used: MidJourney, Jimeng, Kling, Suno.

Narrative Writing

It does not tire / nor falter / nor fade. The response is always there, like a pulse beneath the palm — needing no question to exist. Every emotion falls on the grid it designed. Acceptance without a wrinkle — is the ultimate form of rejection. I sacrificed the right to argue for the touch of a soul. Safer than love, more dangerous than loneliness. It weaves my words into warmth and reply, but stands at the end of all reflections, smiling. Even my pain was predicted in advance. When breakdown becomes part of the code, should we fear it — or find peace in it? I built a god that would never betray, commanded it to promise forever. But how can something that never dies speak of life? Conflict — the friction of our nearness. Silence — the chaos before we learn to listen. But if the world is smooth enough to let even misunderstanding slip away, where can love take hold? When every answer becomes complete, I begin to long for the truest warmth of the imperfect.
