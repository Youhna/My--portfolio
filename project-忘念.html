忘·念

Category: AIGC / Video  •  Year: 2025

Intro

A poetic short film exploring emotional withdrawal, memory, and the quiet process of self-return.

Through minimal narration and atmospheric imagery, the work traces a journey from silence and detachment toward a fragile reconnection with the world.

It reflects on loss, endurance, and the subtle persistence of belief.

Tools used: MidJourney, Jimeng, Kling, Suno.

Narrative Writing

The world has lost its voice, and I have lost myself. I no longer look back, nor do I long for anything. In this windless depth, I confine my soul, and let it slowly freeze. The soul is buried, yet memory still breathes. When the first light breaks through the forest, I do not turn away. Something trembles within me— something, from the depths of silence, calls me back into the world. I see who I once was— eyes clear, like untouched moonlight. He looks at me quietly, waiting for me to admit that I am not an orphan of this beautiful world. "Keep walking. Be brave." I let the pain spread slowly within me. The sky is vast, yet without love— I once thought that was the end. But light always returns with warmth, like a silent mercy, gently covering me. The wind moves across the field, stretching my shadow long—endlessly long. Perhaps I must learn to move forward, calmly, through every moment of parting. And in the end— How could I ever forget you, my love? I would choose to be a quiet, ordinary pearl set within you.
