Reality: Echoes carved in silence

Category: AIGC / Video  •  Year: 2025

Intro

——Dedicated to all souls who seek weight in nothingness.

Tools used: MidJourney,Jimeng,Doubao,Kling,Suno.

Narrative Writing

They said reality was cast in iron, yet all I could ever touch—was mist. They handed me an iron mask, calling it a gift for growing up. I practiced the arc of my smile, again and again, until calluses bloomed on my cheeks, fitting the mask perfectly. Look— the cracks had begun back then. Pain, the very first miracle. When my blood seeped into the cracks, nothingness finally gained its weight. In those years, deep into the night, I often heard heavy footsteps approaching from afar. Until one day, I realized— it was only my own heartbeat, hauling the weight of the entire world inside my hollow chest. How laughable— that we tear each other apart, all for the applause that never truly existed. How absurd— that we dance with shackles on our feet, and call it measuring the boundaries of freedom. So why not — Escape completely, from our rotting shells, from the insoluble riddles of life? "Grasp the light, and you shall become light." Yet the light slipped through my fingers, leaving behind a deeper shadow. We are all transparent vessels, holding the fleeting moment, leaking the eternal. Someday, we will understand: Regret is that the moonlight cannot illuminate certain corners. And life— needs such shadows to define the shape of light. Now I understand: the elephant never truly left. We simply grew used to lowering our heads. Better then— to love the gaps where the wind slips through. For it is through those cracks that the eternal wind passes through our transient bodies. If one day I leave this world, please carve the coordinates of the crack onto my tombstone— it shall become the homeward beacon for all wandering souls.
